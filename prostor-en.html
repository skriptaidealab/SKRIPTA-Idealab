SPACE RENTAL

SKRIPTA
Space

SKRIPTA is a creative space in the centre of Sarajevo, designed for workshops, education, meetings and events that bring people and ideas together. It's not coworking — while your event is on, the space is yours alone.

Send an inquiry
WHY SKRIPTA

Not a rented hall. Not coworking.

  • Guaranteed privacy — the space isn't shared with strangers while your event is on.
  • Design with a story — almost all the furniture is recycled and restored, with work by industrial designers on display.
  • An unconferencing and co-creation approach — the space is designed for work and ideas, not just renting four walls.
  • City centre — 100 m from the OHR, 500 m from embassies and BiH institutions.
LOCATION

Sarajevo city centre

The space is located at Emerika Bluma 9, right in the city centre — 100 metres from the OHR and 500 metres from embassies and BiH institutions. Two free parking spots are available next to the space.

Access is adapted for people with reduced mobility.

EQUIPMENT & SERVICES

What's included in the space

Our space is designed to support your ideas, whether you're running a workshop, a meeting, a training or a creative team day. At SKRIPTA everything is adaptable — from the seating layout to the atmosphere — because we believe good energy is half the job.

We offer:

  • 100 m² of space
  • Flexible, modular seating layout to match your group's needs
  • The latest audio-video equipment for presentations, hybrid meetings and video conferencing
  • 75" and 65" Smart TVs
  • Fast internet and Wi-Fi access for all participants
  • Two glass boards for writing and storyboarding
  • Flipchart
  • Printer and photocopier
  • Two free parking spots
  • Kitchen and dining area, with 2 professional espresso machines
  • Technical assistance, printing materials, help with participant registration, catering service and other needs by arrangement
  • Easier access for people with reduced mobility
