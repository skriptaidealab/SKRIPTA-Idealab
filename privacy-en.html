Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by SKRIPTA IdeaLab, Emerika Bluma 9, Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina. Contact: info@skripta.ba, +387 62 307 885. We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

What data we collect

The site has no forms, user accounts or payments. We receive data in two ways:

  • When you contact us. If you email or call us, or message us on social media, we receive what you choose to share, such as your name, contact details, organisation and event details.
  • Visit statistics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies, does not track individual visitors and builds no profiles. We only see aggregate data such as visit counts, top pages, country, device type and referrer.

Why we use data

We use enquiry data to reply to you, arrange space rental or collaboration and keep in touch. We use aggregate statistics to improve the site. We do not sell your data or use it for automated decision-making.

Legal basis

We process data based on your request or consent (when you contact us), to prepare and perform a contract (rentals, services), and our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used and keeping it secure.

Who else processes data

The site is hosted and protected by Cloudflare, Inc., which processes technical data such as IP addresses when delivering pages, for security and performance. Email runs on Microsoft services. Links to Instagram and TikTok lead to external sites whose privacy policies we do not control.

How long we keep data

We keep enquiry data as long as needed for communication and any resulting collaboration, and as long as legal obligations require. After that we delete it.

Your rights

Under the Law on Personal Data Protection of Bosnia and Herzegovina you may request access to your data, correction, deletion, and object to processing. Send requests to info@skripta.ba. If you believe your rights have been violated, you may contact the Personal Data Protection Agency in Bosnia and Herzegovina.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always the one published on this page, with its last updated date.
