FAQ

Before you book

Is this a café where it is enough to order a drink?

No. You pay the chosen tariff for using a workspace, and VAT is included in the price. Coffee or tea is free.

What do you book in the lounge zone?

The lounge is for groups of 2 to 4 people. There is no separate desk, so the price is lower.

Is the booking confirmed straight away?

No. Your request reaches us, and the booking is confirmed only when we email you a confirmation. Until then the slot is not guaranteed.

How and when do I pay?

On arrival, by cash or card. We do not ask for payment in advance, card details or a user profile.

Do I need a membership?

No. There is no membership or long-term lease. You book only the slot you need, and FLEXI packages exist for regular guests.

I want the whole space for a team or event. What do I do?

Send an inquiry on the Whole-space rental page with the date, duration, number of participants and type of use.

Does your team need
the whole space?

Workshops, trainings, meetings, strategic planning and team work days. Send an inquiry with the date and number of participants.
